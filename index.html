FAQ

Questions clinics ask us.

Is it really free? Any hidden costs? ＋

Completely free. Dispensing, delivery — and if we post it, the Express Post is free too. Prescriber bag medicines are covered by the PBS, so there is no payment involved for your clinic at any point.

Can we upload a script with just one item? ＋

You can — but because we pay the Express Post ourselves, a 1–2 item order may not be able to be fulfilled: the postage can outweigh the order. Batching 3–5 items or more on the one upload means we can ship it free, every time. Need a single item urgently? Call the pharmacy and we'll sort it directly.

Do we need to register or verify our clinic? ＋

No sign-up required. Everything we need — clinic, prescriber and address details — is already on your Dr's Bag script. If we ever need anything more, a pharmacist contacts you directly.

What if we're nowhere near one of your pharmacies? ＋

We ship Australia-wide. Your order is securely packaged and sent free by Australia Post Express with tracking updated daily — next business day in metro areas, up to 3 business days interstate or regional.

What happens if an item is out of stock? ＋

We work with Australia's largest suppliers, so we'll first source it elsewhere. In a genuine nationwide shortage, a pharmacist calls you with the expected restock date — you choose whether to wait or drop the item from the order.

What if a posted order arrives damaged? ＋

We follow strict packaging guidelines, but in the unlikely event something arrives damaged, we reship your order immediately — free of charge.

How is our information handled? ＋

Uploads run through a secure, end-to-end encrypted submission form integrated with document-signing technology — the same platform trusted by major health organisations. Your data is used to fill the order and never retained.
